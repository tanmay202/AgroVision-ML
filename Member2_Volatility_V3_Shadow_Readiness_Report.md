An exhaustive independent forensic audit and empirical validation of the **Volatility V3 experimental system** was conducted to verify its statistical validity, generalization properties, probability calibration, and readiness for prospective shadow deployment.

### Core Audit Conclusions
1. **Target Engineering Repaired:** The fatal calendar horizon flaw of V2 (uncontrolled row shifts reaching 86 days in holdout and 13.8 years in training) is **completely resolved**. Forward price excursions are strictly bounded by calendar windows of at most 7.0 and 14.0 days.
2. **Defensible 14-Day Pooled Advantage:** On the 1-year retrospective holdout ($N=20,329$, 56 mandis), **V3 XGBoost (Balanced)** outperforms both Calendar Persistence and production V2 on pooled Macro F1 (**0.5799** vs **0.5655** vs **0.5567**) and boosts HIGH volatility recall from **44.57%** (V2) to **56.68%** (V3), slashing HIGH-to-LOW misclassifications by 761 cases.

4. **Resolution of the Brier vs ECE Paradox:** The observation that 14D Isotonic calibration improves ECE (0.0410 to 0.0325) while worsening Brier score (0.5122 to 0.5137) was investigated down to machine-level predictions. The primary cause is an **underlying training cohort mismatch**: the uncalibrated score was generated by Champion Raw (trained on all data $< 2023$, 291k rows), whereas the prefit Isotonic calibrator wrapped a base model trained on data $< 2021$ (251k rows, 2 fewer years of data). When evaluating the **exact same base model**, Isotonic calibration **improves Brier score from 0.515408 to 0.513769** (-0.001639).
5. **Zero-Leakage Calibration Architecture:** Calibrators were fitted strictly on Fold 3 validation data ($2021\text{-}01\text{-}27$ to $2023\text{-}01\text{-}26$) and evaluated on retrospective holdout ($2023\text{-}01\text{-}27$ to $2024\text{-}01\text{-}27$) with 570 boundary rows purged, ensuring zero temporal leakage.
6. **Unseen-Mandi Uncertainty:** In 8 completely withheld mandis ($N=2,656$), pooled Macro F1 favors ML (0.5645 vs 0.5423), but per-mandi metrics reveal ML wins in only **2 of 8 mandis (25%)**, with an average per-mandi deficit of $\bar{d} = -0.0264$ ($p = 0.0821$, 95% CI $[-0.0571, +0.0044]$). Pooled results are skewed by two large mandis (Kalipur and Uluberia, comprising 45.3% of test rows).
7. **Production Protection Confirmed:** All production model artifacts remain 100% bit-for-bit identical to baseline states. Zero production routing changes or artifact overwrites have occurred.

## 4. Probability Calibration Investigation & Brier Score Resolution

### 4.1 The Brier vs ECE Paradox Stated
In the initial research report, calibration metrics on the 14-day holdout indicated:
- **Raw XGBoost (Uncalibrated):** Multiclass Brier = **0.5122**, Macro ECE = **0.0410**
- **Isotonic Regression:** Multiclass Brier = **0.5137**, Macro ECE = **0.0325**
This presented an apparent paradox: why does Isotonic Regression improve calibration error (ECE reduced by 20.7%), yet worsen the strictly proper Brier score by +0.0015?

### 4.2 Forensic Investigation & Root Cause Discovery
By executing an in-memory inspection of the serialized artifact `rice_volatility_v3_14d_model.pkl` and tracing data lineage through [`train_and_save_v3_champion.py`](file:///d:/MCA/SEM3/AgroVision-ML/member2-price-volatility/experiments/v3/train_and_save_v3_champion.py#L114-L140):

```python
# Lines 114-135: Base model trained on pre-2021 data, calibrated on 2021-2023 validation
base_cal_model.fit(cal_train_df[V3_FEATURE_COLS], cal_train_df[target_col])
calibrator_iso = CalibratedClassifierCV(base_cal_model, method="isotonic", cv="prefit")
calibrator_iso.fit(cal_val_df[V3_FEATURE_COLS], cal_val_df[target_col])
```

1. **Underlying Model Mismatch:**
   - The reported "Uncalibrated Raw" metric (0.5122) was generated by `model_v3_champion`, which was trained on **all pre-2023 data** (291,728 samples).
   - The `calibrator_isotonic` wrapped `base_cal_model`, which was trained strictly on **pre-2021 data** (251,862 samples, representing **2 fewer years of training history**).
2. **Empirical Verification on Identical Models:**
   Evaluating both raw and calibrated predictions from the **exact same model** (`base_cal_model` trained $< 2021$):
   - `base_cal_model` Raw Brier Score: **0.515408**
   - `base_cal_model` + Isotonic Regression Brier Score: **0.513769**
   - **Delta on the SAME Model:** **-0.001639 (TRUE IMPROVEMENT)**
   - Conclusion: Isotonic calibration **strictly improves Brier score** when evaluated on the model it actually calibrated. The apparent degradation was an artifact of comparing the calibrated pre-2021 model against an uncalibrated pre-2023 champion that benefited from 40,000 additional training observations.
3. **Validation vs Holdout Distribution Shift:**
   - In Fold 3 Validation (2021–2023, where the calibrator was fit): HIGH class prevalence was **39.22%**.
   - In Holdout (2023–2024): HIGH class prevalence dropped to **36.02%** (-3.20%), while LOW rose from 39.19% to **41.80%** (+2.61%).
   - Because raw XGBoost on 14D was already well-calibrated for HIGH volatility (raw ECE = 2.82%), fitting the step function on 2021–2023 data slightly shifted HIGH predictions upward, yielding a holdout HIGH ECE of 4.12%. However, LOW ECE dropped from 5.58% to 3.43% and MEDIUM ECE dropped from 3.90% to 2.19%, resulting in a net Macro ECE reduction from 4.10% to **3.25%**.

### 4.3 Independence of Fitting and Evaluation Sets
- Calibrator training set: Fold 3 validation data ($2021\text{-}01\text{-}27$ to $2023\text{-}01\text{-}26$, $N=39,726$).
- Calibrator evaluation set: Independent holdout ($2023\text{-}01\text{-}27$ to $2024\text{-}01\text{-}27$, $N=20,329$).
- Non-overlapping boundary purging: 570 boundary records were purged. Zero leakage verified.

## 7. Prediction Timestamps, Missing-Data & Probability Validation

### 7.1 Target Calendar Windows & Timestamp Verification
- Forecast origin: Observation timestamp $t$ (`Reported Date`).
- Horizon target window: Strictly $[t, t+14]$ calendar days.
- Excursion calculation: $\text{pct\_range} = (\max_{s \in [t, t+14]} P_s - \min_{s \in [t, t+14]} P_s) / P_t \times 100\%$.
- Verification: Across all 312,947 observations, the maximum elapsed calendar time between origin $t$ and any observation used in the target is **strictly $\le 14.0$ days**.
- Zero Trading Days: If no trades occur in $(t, t+14]$, the record receives `NaN` and is excluded from training and evaluation.

### 7.2 Probability Outputs & Extreme Missing-Data Behavior
1. **Probability Vector Invariant:**
   - Both uncalibrated XGBoost (`multi:softprob`) and calibrated models (`CalibratedClassifierCV`) output valid probability triples:
     $$p_{\text{LOW}} + p_{\text{MED}} + p_{\text{HIGH}} = 1.00000 \pm 10^{-6}$$
2. **Missing Feature Handling:**
   - XGBoost natively routes NaNs through default branch splits.
   - Empirical test: When features contain missing lags or rolling statistics, calibrated probabilities remain valid and bounded.
   - **Critical Vulnerability Identified:** When **100% of input features are NaN** (e.g. empty history submitted to API), XGBoost defaults to predicting **73.8% to 81.0% HIGH volatility**.
   - **Operational Mandate:** The production API must enforce explicit history validation (`InsufficientHistoryError` requiring $\ge 14$ valid historical observations). Empty or severely degraded histories must return HTTP 422 or fallback to persistence baseline, rather than permitting the tree model to default to false HIGH alarms.

### 8.1 Architectural Rules & Hard Invariants
1. **Production Isolation:**
   - Active production routing remains 100% committed to Calendar Persistence.
   - V3 predictions must **never** be exposed to users, mobile apps, or extension APIs as confirmed decisions.
2. **Deterministic Forecast Identity:**
   $$\text{forecast\_id} = \text{SHA256}(\text{market} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{variety} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{origin\_date} \mathbin{\Vert} 14 \mathbin{\Vert} \text{"v3\_14d"})$$
   Guarantees strict idempotency and eliminates duplicate records.
3. **Temporal Evaluation Gating:**
   - Forecasts remain in `PENDING` status for **at least 14 full calendar days**.
   - No forecast is evaluated until $T_{\text{current}} \ge \text{origin\_date} + 14 \text{ days}$.
   - If zero trading occurred during the 14-day window, status transitions to `WINDOW_EMPTY` (excluded from scoring).
4. **Prospective Cohort Quarantine:**
   - All shadow predictions must carry `cohort = 'PROSPECTIVE'`.
   - Never combine retrospective holdout data with live prospective records.
5. **Cluster-Aware Statistical Tracking:**
   - Evaluation reports must calculate:
     - Disaggregated per-mandi win rates.
     - Paired cluster $t$-test and clustered bootstrap 95% confidence intervals.
     - Multiclass Brier score and Expected Calibration Error (ECE).
